## Binding and coverage prerequisites

- **Keep the two hash domains distinct.** Public selected-source grants use the full snapshot exposure hash of `{id,text,provenance}`: [brain_loop.source_exposure_hash](../../../cognitive_castle/brain_loop.py#L186), also [semantic_intents.source_exposure_hash](../../../cognitive_castle/semantic_intents.py#L9). Vector `source_hash` is the exact text hash. Currently [LanceVectorStore.nearest](../../../cognitive_castle/lance_vector_store.py#L578) filters its misleadingly named allowed_source_hashes argument on the **text** field at line617. Public exposure-grant lists must not be passed into that argument. The future candidate boundary must explicitly authorize the exposure domain before ranking/limits and retain exact hydration rechecks.
- [query_exact](../../../cognitive_castle/semantic_indexer.py#L243) currently requests `generation=None` at line272. Bind active registered generation, vector profile/input/source/exposure hashes, and relevant occurrence scope. Preserve BrainAPI's post-work policy_epoch check; scope changes or selected grants must not expose a candidate through another provider, preview or context route.
- Apply trusted workspace/wing/room/policy restrictions before each provider's ranking/limit and fusion. Legacy explicit modes do not even accept workspace; copying the whole legacy search path would not satisfy this requirement. KG may remain derived coordination/evidence, never a second canonical-body authority.
- Exact success, semantic indexing, lexical/KG availability and full-corpus reconciliation are separate facts. The current SQLite catalog coverage or migration semantic_pending count does not prove Lance/vector completeness. An unavailable semantic stage stays unavailable/incomplete; no hidden SQLite canonical hydration, lexical-only success relabeling, or automatic legacy-corpus fallback. Retained originals/rollback stores remain preserved.
